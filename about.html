ABOUT US

CRAFTING QUALITY WOOD FURNITURE FOR OVER 35 YEARS

Our Story

Founded in 1985 by master carpenter James Harrison, Pinecrest Lumber Co. began as a small workshop in the heart of pine country. What started as a one-man operation crafting custom furniture for local families has grown into a trusted name in handcrafted wood furniture.

For three generations, we've maintained the same commitment to quality that James instilled from day one: use the finest local pine, employ traditional joinery techniques, and never compromise on craftsmanship. Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the pride of North Carolina's rich woodworking heritage.

Today, we're proud to serve customers across the country while remaining true to our roots as a family-owned business. Our team of 25 skilled artisans continues to handcraft each piece in our expanded 20,000 square foot facility, combining time-honored techniques with modern innovations.
